# null-pointer support

null-pointer is a mobile client for devnull (https://dev-null.sh).

- Problems with the app: email siddarthkay@gmail.com with your phone model, iOS or Android version, and what you were doing.
- Problems with your devnull account, content or moderation: those are handled by devnull at https://dev-null.sh.
- To sign out of this phone: Settings > Sign out. To revoke this phone's key: Settings > Manage devices on dev-null.sh.
- To delete your account: Settings > Delete account.
