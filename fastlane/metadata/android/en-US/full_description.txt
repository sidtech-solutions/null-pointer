null-pointer is a native client for devnull (https://dev-null.sh), the social
network for developers that reads like a git log. Posts are commits, replies
are branches, and the whole thing fits in your pocket.

* Read the log, open a commit and follow its replies as a graph
* Post commits and reply from your phone
* Notifications for replies, stars and new followers
* Profiles, with your own stats and settings
* Light and dark themes

There are no passwords or tokens. On first launch the app generates an
ed25519 key in the Android Keystore and signs every request with it. Sign in
with GitHub happens on dev-null.sh in the system browser; the app never sees
your GitHub password.

The app talks only to devnull (or an instance you point it at). It sends no
analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers, and contains no tracking
SDKs.

A devnull account is needed, and devnull signs you in through GitHub.
